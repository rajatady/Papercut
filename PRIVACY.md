# Privacy Policy — Papercut

**Last updated:** February 20, 2026

Papercut is a free, open-source iOS app for browsing and summarizing arXiv research papers. Your privacy is fundamental to how Papercut is built.

## Data Collection

**Papercut does not collect any data.** Specifically:

- No user accounts or login
- No analytics or telemetry
- No tracking of any kind
- No crash reporting
- No advertising
- No cookies or fingerprinting

## Network Requests

The only network requests Papercut makes are to the **arXiv API** (`export.arxiv.org`) to fetch paper metadata (titles, authors, abstracts, categories). These requests are standard HTTP GET requests and do not include any user-identifying information.

## On-Device Processing

AI-powered summaries are generated entirely on-device using Apple Foundation Models. No paper content or summaries are sent to any server.

## Local Storage

All data — including your followed categories, bookmarked papers, cached summaries, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

Papercut does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs other than the public arXiv API.

## Children's Privacy

Papercut does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
